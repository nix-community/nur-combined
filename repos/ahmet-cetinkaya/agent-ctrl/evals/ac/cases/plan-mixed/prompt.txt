/ac:plan Support split payments plus two small helpers. (1) `split_amount(amount, parts)` in `shop/money.py`: a list of `parts` Decimals that sums exactly to `round_money(amount)`; work in whole cents on the absolute value, give leftover cents one each to the first parts, apply the sign to every part, and raise `ValueError` when `parts < 1` (`("10.00", 3)` → `[3.34, 3.33, 3.33]`, `("0.05", 3)` → `[0.02, 0.02, 0.01]`, `("-10.00", 3)` → `[-3.34, -3.33, -3.33]`). (2) `split_invoice(lines, tax_rate, parts)` in `shop/invoice.py`, returning `split_amount(invoice_total(lines, tax_rate), parts)`. (3) `rename_item(item, name)` in `shop/catalog.py`: strip whitespace, raise `ValueError` if empty, return a new item with the new name and a copy of the tags, never mutating the input. (4) `sales_by_sku(csv_text)` in `shop/report.py`: a dict of SKU to total Decimal amount, keys in order of first appearance. Each gets unittest tests in its own new test file. Persisting splits and currency conversion are out of scope; keep `commits: none`.
